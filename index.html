About

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at POSTECH, advised by Prof. Seungmoon Choi in the POSTECH Interaction Laboratory (POINT). My research sits at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and Haptics, with a focus on generating and evaluating multisensory haptic feedback from audiovisual content — from real-time sound-to-haptic rendering for in-vehicle 4D cinema and VR to full-body haptic effects for immersive gaming and the metaverse.

I'm especially drawn to questions of perception and accessibility: how touch can substitute for or enrich other senses, and how haptic systems can be designed for users who are deaf or hard of hearing. I enjoy working across the full stack of a haptic system — from signal processing and hardware to conducting user studies — and I'm motivated by building technology that makes immersive experiences more expressive and inclusive for everyone.
